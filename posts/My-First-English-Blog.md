# My first English Blog
**2026-02-14**

I have learned English for 13 years. English is a compulsary lesson in China's education system. But it's not an important issue for village students. The four years learning experience in primary school is like just knowing basic English letters and simple greeting.

This blog was designed for Chinese airicles about my life and thoughts at first. As a native Chinese, I even cann't write logical Chinese articles. I never write any long articles in English except for exam papers and graduation thesis. I prepared for IELTS for few weeks, but only practiced listedning and reading skills. Writing in English is definitely a big problem for me, which needs a lot of practice.

I planed to publish Chinese articles in this website like I did many times before. It's not that interesting to write something I wrote again, which is why I decide to write in English.

There is always a saying about learning English goes, to achieve native speaker level, learners need to know how to think in English. Writing is exactly a right way to think in English. It's impossible for me to talk in English at home, even talking with AI making me wierd for parents.

As you can see, all vocabularies I used in this article are basic and basic words. There is no doubt that I might only know 3000 English words, same level as a high school students. Whatever, even just typing in English makes me feel confident, no matter what rubbish I am producing.

I have to admint, English typing is much faster than Chinese, which is also keyboard originally designed for. But typing fast doesn't mean writing good, just means translating my simple ideas into English is too easy. For simple thoughts person like me, basic words is enough to express.

While writing in English, I'll try to learn HTML and CSS to make this page looks better, at least like a modern website, not 90s style web. At the beginning, it's an good excuse to say this design is last century style.
