# Why I Need to Write in English #

***2026-2-27***

Native language (or mothertongue) is definitely the best language for human to write, making thoughts clearly expressed, most effective for thinking. Why do we need to learn a new language? Or I should ask, in this generation that AI can replace most jobs like translating, is it still necessary to learn a new language?

Plenty of Chinese students hold this point: it's useless to study English at school, of which I agree of some. However, only studying on books is not learning a language at all.
